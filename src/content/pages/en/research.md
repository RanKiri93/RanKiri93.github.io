### Operator systems and subhomogeneity

A $C^*$-algebra is $N$-subhomogeneous if all of its irreducible representations are matrices of size at most $N$. In my paper I extend this notion to operator systems, with boundary representations taking the place of irreducible representations, and use these tools to classify the operator systems generated by pairs of $q$-commuting unitaries.

### Arveson's essential normality conjecture

On the Drury–Arveson space, multiplication by the coordinates $z_1, \dots, z_d$ gives a $d$-tuple of commuting operators. Arveson conjectured that when these are compressed to the quotients defined by homogeneous polynomial ideals, they commute with their adjoints up to compact operators: they are essentially normal. The conjecture links operator theory with algebraic geometry, and it is still open in general.

### Von Neumann–type inequalities

Von Neumann's inequality says that for every contraction $T$ and polynomial $p$, $\|p(T)\| \le \max_{|z| \le 1} |p(z)|$. It holds for pairs of commuting contractions (Andô) but already fails for triples (Varopoulos). I look at which versions of such inequalities survive in other settings, and what they say about the operators involved.
