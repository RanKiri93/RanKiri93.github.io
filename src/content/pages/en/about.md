I'm a PhD student in mathematics at the Technion, advised by Prof. Orr Moshe Shalit. I'm interested in the geometric structure of operator systems, and in how it relates to the properties of these systems and of the algebras they generate. Lately I've also been studying operators that arise around William Arveson's essential normality conjecture, and possible new versions of von Neumann–type inequalities.

Alongside research I teach a lot at the Technion, both leading recitations and lecturing math courses, and I care about improving how mathematics is taught, at the Technion and beyond, on the technological side as well as the pedagogical one.
